Two days covers Kyoto's highlights if you split the city by geography instead of chasing a checklist: east on day one, west and north on day two.

## The Short Version

- **Day 1, east:** Fushimi Inari at dawn, Kiyomizudera, the Higashiyama walk, evening in Gion.
- **Day 2, west and north:** Arashiyama's bamboo grove and Tenryu-ji in the morning, Kinkaku-ji in the afternoon.
- **Start early both days.** 6:00am at Fushimi Inari, 7:30am for Arashiyama. By mid-morning both are packed.
- **Book Day 1 dinner before you leave home.** Gion's kaiseki and obanzai restaurants fill weeks ahead on weekends. Walk-ins only work for ramen and casual places.
- **No car needed.** An IC card loaded with ¥3,000–¥5,000 covers both days.

## Day 1: Fushimi Inari, Higashiyama, Gion

| Time | Stop | Getting there and cost |
|---|---|---|
| 06:00 | **[Fushimi Inari](/blog/sights/fushimi-inari).** Walk the lower torii tunnels for 30–45 min. The best ones are in the first 20 min, so skip the summit. Leave by 7:30am. | JR Nara Line from Kyoto Station to Inari, 5 min, ¥150. Free entry |
| 08:00 | **Breakfast.** Inoda Coffee near Karasuma-Oike (opens 7am), a convenience store, or the cafés on the Kiyomizudera slope from 8:30am. | Keihan Line north from Fushimi-Inari. ¥400–¥1,200 |
| 09:00 | **[Kiyomizudera](/blog/sights/kiyomizudera).** 60–75 min. Start with the wooden stage and its view over the city. | Bus 206 to Gojozaka, or taxi. ¥500 |
| 10:30 | **[Higashiyama walk](/blog/neighborhoods/higashiyama).** Downhill through Sannenzaka and Ninenzaka, past Kodaiji (¥800, optional) and Maruyama Park to Yasaka Shrine. 90 min. | On foot |
| 12:30 | **Lunch in Gion.** An obanzai set lunch is the most Kyoto choice. | ¥1,500–¥2,500 |
| 14:00 | **[Gion](/blog/neighborhoods/gion) walk.** Hanamikoji and the Shimbashi canal while the streets are quiet. 90 min. | Free |
| 17:00 | **Nishiki Market.** Snacks and pickle stalls under a covered arcade. Some stalls close as early as 4pm and most by 6pm, so do not arrive any later. | 15 min walk west. ¥0–¥800 |
| 19:00 | **Dinner, then Gion after dark.** The lanterns are at their best from 7pm to 9pm. | ¥3,000–¥6,000 |

- **Ishibei-koji** is a quiet side lane off the Higashiyama route with no souvenir stalls, a 5-minute detour. Photography is not allowed there.
- **Stop for matcha soft serve** on Ninenzaka. You have time.

## Day 2: Arashiyama, Kinkaku-ji

| Time | Stop | Getting there and cost |
|---|---|---|
| 07:30 | **Arashiyama.** [Bamboo grove](/blog/sights/arashiyama-bamboo) before 9am, then Tenryu-ji garden (¥500), Togetsukyo Bridge and the river. 3 hours. Area guide: [Arashiyama](/blog/neighborhoods/arashiyama). | JR Sagano Line from Kyoto Station to Saga-Arashiyama, about 15 min, ¥240 |
| 11:00 | **Early lunch.** Cafés and lunch spots line the street between the bridge and the station. A full tofu course is ¥2,000–¥3,500. Those restaurants open at 11:30am, so book or arrive on time. | ¥1,000–¥2,000 |
| 13:00 | **[Kinkaku-ji](/blog/sights/kinkakuji).** A one-way circuit around the pond, 30–40 min. Add the Ryoan-ji rock garden (¥600), about 20 min away on foot or a short taxi ride. | JR from Saga-Arashiyama to Emmachi, then bus 204 or 205 to Kinkakuji-michi, 40–50 min in all, or taxi. ¥500 |
| 15:30 | **Optional: [Nijo Castle](/blog/sights/nijo-castle).** Nightingale floors and painted palace rooms. Last entry is 4:00pm and it closes at 5:00pm, so choose this or Ryoan-ji, not both. | Tozai subway line. ¥1,300 |
| 18:00 | **Dinner in Gion or Pontocho.** In summer, Pontocho restaurants set up *yuka* terraces over the Kamo River. | ¥2,500–¥5,000 |
