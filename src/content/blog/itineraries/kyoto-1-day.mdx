One day in Kyoto works if you stay on the east side and start before sunrise: Fushimi Inari at dawn, Higashiyama in the morning, Gion after dark.

## The Short Version

- **Be at Fushimi Inari by 6:00am.** The gates are empty at dawn and shoulder to shoulder by 10am. The first JR train toward Nara leaves Kyoto Station around 5:30am.
- **Stay east.** Fushimi Inari, Higashiyama and Gion fit in one day. Arashiyama does not.
- **Use trains and taxis, not buses,** between 9am and 5pm on weekends and holidays.
- **Rest at 4pm.** You will walk 15,000–18,000 steps, and Gion is at its best after dark.
- **Budget ¥6,100–¥9,600 per person** for entry fees, three meals and transit. With a better dinner, ¥10,000–¥14,000.

Notes that save the day:

- **The north loop is optional.** Skip it on holidays or when you are tired. [Nijo Castle](https://maps.google.com/?q=Nijo+Castle+Kyoto) is the easier swap: subway access, ¥1,300.
- **Keep moving on Sannenzaka.** No tripods in the lanes. [Ishibei-koji](https://maps.google.com/?q=Ishibei-koji+Kyoto) is a quiet 5-minute detour, with no photography allowed.
- **At Nishiki, eat at the stall,** not while walking.
- **Never photograph, call out to or follow** maiko and geisha in Gion.
- **Carry ¥3,000 in cash** for small temples, market stalls and coin lockers.
- **In cherry blossom season,** start 30 minutes earlier everywhere. See [Kyoto Cherry Blossom Spots](/blog/seasonal/kyoto-cherry-blossom-spots).

<TipBox type="warning" title="DON'T ADD MORE">
One area per half-day: Higashiyama in the morning, the north or central Kyoto in the afternoon, Gion in the evening. Adding Arashiyama turns the day into commuting.
</TipBox>

## If It Rains

- Swap the Fushimi Inari walk for the covered Nishiki Market.
- Swap Kiyomizu-dera for Nijo Castle, which is mostly indoors.
- Add the [Kyoto National Museum](https://maps.google.com/?q=Kyoto+National+Museum) or the covered [Teramachi](https://maps.google.com/?q=Teramachi+Kyoto) and [Shinkyogoku](https://maps.google.com/?q=Shinkyogoku+Kyoto) arcades.
- Keep the evening. Gion on wet stone under lanterns is worth the umbrella. More ideas: [Rainy Day in Kyoto](/blog/itineraries/rainy-day-kyoto).
