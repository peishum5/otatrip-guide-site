Three days is the sweet spot for a first visit to Kyoto: east, west and north without rushing, plus a spare day for central Kyoto or Nara.

## The Short Version

- **Day 1, east:** Fushimi Inari before 8am, Kiyomizudera, the Higashiyama walk, Gion.
- **Day 2, west and north:** Arashiyama in the morning, Kinkaku-ji and Ryoan-ji in the afternoon.
- **Day 3, your choice:** Nijo Castle and the Philosopher's Path, or a day trip to Nara.
- **The one fixed point is Fushimi Inari at dawn on Day 1.** It costs nothing, and the first JR train toward Nara leaves Kyoto Station around 5:20am.
- **Use an IC card, not a day pass.** A pass only pays off at about five rides in a day.

## Day 1: Eastern Kyoto

| Time | Stop | Getting there and cost |
|---|---|---|
| 06:00 | **[Fushimi Inari](/blog/sights/fushimi-inari).** The lower torii tunnels, then up to Yotsutsuji junction for the city view. 90 min. | JR Nara Line from Kyoto Station to Inari, 5 min, ¥150. Free entry |
| 09:00 | **Kiyomizudera and the [Higashiyama walk](/blog/neighborhoods/higashiyama).** 60–75 min in the temple, then downhill through Sannenzaka and Ninenzaka to Yasaka Shrine. 3–4 hours. Tour groups arrive after 11am. | Bus or taxi to Kiyomizu-michi. ¥500 |
| 13:00 | **Lunch and Gion.** An obanzai set lunch, then Hanamikoji and Shimbashi, quieter once the midday groups have gone. Optional: Nishiki Market, 15 min west. | ¥1,200–¥1,800 |
| 18:00 | **Dinner in Gion or Pontocho.** Maiko are most often seen from 5pm to 7pm on weekdays near Hanamikoji, just before dinner. | ¥3,000–¥8,000. Reserve anything mid-range and up |

## Day 2: Western and Northern Kyoto

| Time | Stop | Getting there and cost |
|---|---|---|
| 07:30 | **[Arashiyama](/blog/neighborhoods/arashiyama).** Bamboo grove before 9am, then Tenryu-ji garden (¥500), Nonomiya Shrine and Togetsukyo Bridge. With time to spare: Jojakko-ji or Okochi Sanso. 4–4.5 hours. | JR Sagano Line from Kyoto Station to Saga-Arashiyama, about 15 min |
| 13:30 | **[Kinkaku-ji](/blog/sights/kinkakuji) and Ryoan-ji.** 30–40 min on the Golden Pavilion's one-way circuit, then the 15-stone rock garden. 2.5 hours. | Bus from Arashiyama (about 50 min) or taxi. Between the two: about 20 min on foot or a short taxi. ¥500 + ¥600 |
| 18:00 | **Dinner, or the [Gion Night Walk](/tours/gion-sake-walk)** if you have not done it yet. Kawaramachi and Teramachi streets have good mid-range restaurants. | ¥2,000–¥4,000 |

## Day 3: Central Kyoto or Nara

**Option A: stay in the city.**

| Time | Stop | Getting there and cost |
|---|---|---|
| 09:00 | **[Nijo Castle](/blog/sights/nijo-castle).** Nightingale floors and the painted rooms of Ninomaru Palace. Allow 2 hours or more. | Tozai subway line to Nijojo-mae. ¥1,300 |
| 11:30 | **Philosopher's Path and Nanzenji.** A 2 km canal-side walk with Ginkaku-ji (¥500) at the north end and Nanzenji to the south. 2.5 hours. | Nanzenji grounds are free. Sub-temples ¥500–¥600 |
| 14:00 | **Nishiki Market and last shopping.** A late lunch at the market, then the Imperial Palace Park (free) or the Kawaramachi arcades. 2 hours. | Walk or bus from Nanzenji. ¥500–¥2,000 |

**Option B: [Nara day trip](/blog/day-trips/nara-from-kyoto).** About 35 minutes by Kintetsu Limited Express from Kyoto Station. Pick this if temples and deer matter more to you than Nijo Castle.

- Arrive by 9am, before the tour buses. The deer are most active in the morning.
- See Todaiji before 11am.
- Be back in Kyoto by 4pm or 5pm to keep the evening for Gion.
