Kyoto's temples close around sunset, and many visitors go back to the hotel when they do. The old streets are better after dark: the tour groups have left and the lanterns are on.

## The Short Version

- **Temples close between about 4:30pm and 6pm.** After that, Kyoto is streets, shrines and viewpoints.
- **Free and never closed:** Yasaka Shrine, Fushimi Inari, the Gion lanes, Pontocho and the Kamo River.
- **The best walk:** Pontocho, across the river to Gion, then up to the Yasaka Pagoda. About an hour and a half.
- **In spring and autumn,** a handful of temples reopen at night with illuminated gardens. Dates are below.
- **Check your last train.** Through trains to Osaka on the Keihan and Hankyu lines end at 23:15.

## Free, and Open All Night

- **[Gion](/blog/neighborhoods/gion):** Hanamikoji and the Shirakawa canal under lantern light. The teahouses start their evening around 6pm.
- **Pontocho:** a narrow alley of restaurants and bars beside the Kamo River. Walk it once even if you eat elsewhere.
- **[Yasaka Shrine](https://maps.google.com/?q=Yasaka+Shrine+Kyoto):** open 24 hours, at the east end of Shijo Street. The rows of lanterns on the dance stage are the picture to take.
- **Yasaka Pagoda, Ninenzaka and Nene-no-michi:** the [Higashiyama](/blog/neighborhoods/higashiyama) lanes are packed by day and nearly empty by night. Shops are shut, and that is the point.
- **[Fushimi Inari](/blog/sights/fushimi-inari):** open 24 hours and five minutes from Kyoto Station by JR. Stay on the lower, lit part of the gates.
- **The Kamo River:** on warm evenings people sit along the bank between Sanjo and Shijo.

## Night Illuminations

A few temples reopen after dark in spring and autumn. These are the autumn 2026 dates. Spring 2027 dates were not yet published when this was written.

| Where | Autumn 2026 dates | Evening hours | Fee |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kiyomizu-dera | 21–30 Nov | 17:30, last entry 21:00 | ¥500 |
| Kodai-ji | 23 Oct–13 Dec | 17:00, last entry 21:30 | ¥800 |
| Chion-in | 17 Nov–6 Dec | 17:30, last entry 21:00 | ¥800 |
| Shoren-in | 6 Nov–6 Dec | 18:00, last entry 21:30 | ¥1,500 |
| Toji | 31 Oct–13 Dec | 18:00, last entry 21:00 | ¥1,000 |
| Nijo Castle (NAKED meets Nijo-jo) | 23 Oct–5 Dec | 18:00, last entry 21:00 | ¥2,000–¥2,400 |

- **Kiyomizu-dera** opens at night for only ten days, so it is the busiest. Go on a weekday.
- **Kodai-ji** runs the longest and is a short walk from Yasaka Shrine, so it fits into a Gion evening.
- From mid-December to mid-March there are few night openings. See [Kyoto in Winter](/blog/seasonal/kyoto-winter) for what runs instead.

## City Views

- **Kyoto Tower:** ¥1,000 for adults since October 2026. Open 10:00–21:00, last entry 20:30.
- **Kyoto Station, free:** the Skyway on the 10th floor looks north over the city (10:00–22:00), and the Grand Staircase is lit with moving images until 22:00. The rooftop garden is closed for construction from 27 November 2026 until about 2031. More in the [Kyoto Station guide](/blog/plan/kyoto-station-guide).

## What Closes Early

| Place | Closes around |
|---|---|
| Most temples | 16:30–17:00 |
| Kiyomizu-dera | 18:00 |
| [Nishiki Market](/blog/food/nishiki-market) | Most stalls by 17:30–18:00 |
| Most museums | 17:00 |

Plan temples for the morning and keep the evening for walking and eating.

## One Evening, Hour by Hour

| Time | What |
|---|---|
| 17:00 | Yasaka Pagoda and Ninenzaka as the light goes and the shops shut |
| 18:00 | Walk down through Gion to the river. Dinner in Pontocho or an [izakaya](/blog/food/izakaya-etiquette) in Kiyamachi |
| 19:30 | Back across the river: the Shirakawa canal, then Hanamikoji |
| 20:30 | Yasaka Shrine and its lanterns |
| 21:00 | A last drink near Gion-Shijo, or the train home |

<TipBox type="warning" title="GION AT NIGHT: TWO RULES">
<strong>Do not photograph, follow or stop maiko and geisha.</strong> They are on their way to work.<br /><br />
<strong>Stay out of the private side alleys</strong> off Hanamikoji. Residents' signs ban entry and photography there and warn of a ¥10,000 fine. Hanamikoji itself is a public street and stays open.
</TipBox>

## Getting Home

| Line | Last train on a weekday |
|---|---|
| Subway Karasuma Line, Shijo to Kyoto Station | 23:57 |
| JR, Kyoto to Osaka | 23:58 (last Special Rapid 23:29) |
| Keihan, Gion-Shijo to Osaka (Yodoyabashi) | 23:15 |
| Keihan, Gion-Shijo to Demachiyanagi | 0:07 |
| Hankyu, Kyoto-Kawaramachi to Osaka-Umeda | 23:15 |
| City bus 206, Gion to Kyoto Station | 23:33 |

- **Taxis** cost about 20% more between 10pm and 5am. The GO and DiDi apps work in English. See [How to Get Around Kyoto](/blog/plan/transport-kyoto).
- **Staying in Osaka?** After 23:15, JR from Kyoto Station is the only through train left.
