Most visitors give Arashiyama 40 minutes: the bamboo grove, a photo, and back on the train. Stay half a day and you also get one of Japan's finest temple gardens, a hillside villa with city views and a quiet riverside.

## The Short Version

- **Start before 9am on a weekday**, and do the bamboo grove first. Before 8am it is nearly empty.
- **Allow 3–4 hours.** A full day lets you add Jojakko-ji, Okochi Sanso and the scenic railway.
- **Do not skip Tenryu-ji.** The garden costs ¥500 and is the best thing here.
- **Book the Sagano Scenic Railway weeks ahead** if you come in autumn.
- **Eat one block back from Togetsukyo Bridge.** The places facing the bridge are overpriced.

## What to See

<ImageCaption src="/images/blog/inline/arashiyama-togetsukyo-bridge.jpg" alt="Togetsukyo Bridge spanning the Oi River at Arashiyama with forested mountains behind" badge="ARASHIYAMA" caption="Togetsukyo Bridge at first light, before the crowds arrive." />

| Sight | Cost | Why go |
|---|---|---|
| [Bamboo Grove](/blog/sights/arashiyama-bamboo) | Free | The famous 500 m path just north of Tenryu-ji. Go before 8am |
| Tenryu-ji | ¥500 garden, ¥800 with the buildings | A UNESCO World Heritage garden from 1339, with the hills as its backdrop. Allow 30–45 minutes |
| Togetsukyo Bridge | Free | The center of the district and its best-known view. Best in morning light |
| Jojakko-ji | ¥600 | A mossy temple 5 minutes uphill from the grove, with pagoda views and a fraction of the visitors |
| Okochi Sanso Villa | ¥1,000 with matcha and sweets | A film star's hillside estate at the far end of the bamboo path. Terraced gardens, city views, no crowds |
| Sagano Scenic Railway | Book ahead | A small tourist train that runs 7.3 km through the Hozugawa gorge from Torokko Saga Station |

If you add only one detour to the main circuit, make it Jojakko-ji.

<TipBox type="warning" title="BOOK THE SCENIC RAILWAY EARLY">
In peak autumn foliage season (late November) the Sagano Scenic Railway sells out weeks in advance, and walk-up tickets on weekends are effectively gone. Book on the official website as soon as your dates are fixed.
</TipBox>

## The Half-Day Route

From JR Saga-Arashiyama Station, 3–4 hours at an easy pace:

1. Walk 10 minutes to Nonomiya Shrine at the grove entrance.
2. Walk the bamboo grove (15 minutes).
3. Continue to Okochi Sanso (20 minutes if you go in).
4. Come back and enter Tenryu-ji by the north gate, on the bamboo side. You reach the pond garden last, which is the better order. Allow 40 minutes.
5. Leave by the main gate and walk to Togetsukyo Bridge (15 minutes).
6. Walk the riverside path, then return to the station along the main street.

On weekends the main street and the bridge are packed, but the hillside temples stay quieter. The side lanes north of the grove are calm even when the main path is full.

## Eating

- **Sagano-yu:** a former public bath turned casual restaurant, in a beautiful old building.
- **Yudofu:** several restaurants around Tenryu-ji serve *yudofu*, simmered tofu, the classic Arashiyama lunch.
- **Cafes and convenience stores:** along the main street between the bridge and the bamboo area.
