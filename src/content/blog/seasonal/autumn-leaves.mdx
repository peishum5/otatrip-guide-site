Kyoto's autumn colour is easier to predict than its cherry blossoms, and more crowded at the famous gardens. Pick your dates from the forecast, go on a weekday morning, and add one quiet spot.

## The Short Version

- **Aim for late November to early December.** Full colour lasts 10–14 days at any one spot.
- **Go on a weekday morning.** The top spots have 50–60% fewer visitors than on weekends.
- **Eikan-do and Tofukuji are the big two.** Both have queues of an hour or more on peak weekends.
- **Book early.** Hotels 3–6 months ahead, and Rurikoin as soon as its autumn tickets open.
- **Check the forecast before you pay for anything non-refundable.**

## When to Go

- **The sequence:** leaves start to turn in mid-November, peak from late November to early December, and fall through mid-December.
- **The forecast:** the Japan Meteorological Corporation and Tenki.jp publish *koyo* forecasts from September. Trust them from about two weeks out.
- **What moves the date:** an early cold snap brings the peak forward. A warm autumn pushes it later.
- **Best light:** overcast days give even colour, and the 30 minutes after light rain is when the reds look deepest.

## The Famous Spots

| Spot | Why go | Crowds and tips |
|---|---|---|
| **Eikan-do**<br />¥1,500 in autumn | Thousands of maples on a hillside, from the pond garden up to the pagoda. Widely seen as Kyoto's best koyo temple | Multi-hour queues on peak weekends. Be there at 9am opening on a weekday |
| **Tofukuji**<br />¥1,000 | The Tsutenkyo bridge looks down on a valley filled with red maples | The most crowded foliage spot in Kyoto. 1–2 hour queue on peak weekends |
| **Arashiyama**<br />Tenryu-ji garden ¥500 | The river gorge and hillsides turn red and orange. See the [Arashiyama guide](/blog/neighborhoods/arashiyama) | The Sagano Scenic Railway sells out weeks ahead for November weekends |
| **Rurikoin**<br />¥2,000 | A lacquered table reflects the maple garden overhead. In Yase, in the north-east hills | Reservation required in the peak weeks. Open in spring, summer and autumn only |
| **Kiyomizudera**<br />¥500 | The hillside below the wooden stage turns scarlet and gold. See the [Kiyomizudera guide](/blog/sights/kiyomizudera) | Also open for night illumination |
| **Philosopher's Path**<br />Free | A canal walk between Nanzenji and Ginkaku-ji under maple and cherry trees | Quietest early in the morning |

## Quieter Alternatives

| Spot | Why go | Getting there |
|---|---|---|
| **Jojakko-ji**<br />¥600 | A moss-covered pagoda among maples | On the Arashiyama hillside, far quieter than the main area |
| **Daigoji**<br />¥1,500 | A UNESCO site with a five-story pagoda among maples | Tozai subway to Daigo. Much less visited than the central temples |
| **Sanzen-in** | A moss and maple garden around a small hall, at its best in late November | About an hour by bus to Ohara village. The ride keeps the crowds away. Allow half a day |

## Night Illumination
