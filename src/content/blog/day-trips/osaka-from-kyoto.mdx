Osaka is 30 minutes from Kyoto Station for ¥580. It is loud, neon-lit and obsessed with eating, which makes it the sharpest contrast to Kyoto you can get in half a day.

## The Short Version

- **Go for the food and the street life.** If you want more temples, [Nara](/blog/day-trips/nara-from-kyoto) is the better trip.
- **Take the JR Special Rapid:** 30 minutes, ¥580, every 10–15 minutes from Kyoto Station.
- **Staying near Gion or Kawaramachi? Take Hankyu:** 45 minutes, ¥410, no transfer.
- **Skip the Shinkansen.** It stops at Shin-Osaka, which is not central Osaka.
- **Half a day (3–4 hours) covers Dotonbori and Kuromon Market.** Stay for dinner if you can: the canal is best after dark.

## Getting There

| Option | From → To | Time | Fare | Good to know |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **JR Special Rapid (新快速)** *(best for most)* | Kyoto Station → Osaka Station | 30 min | ¥580 | No surcharge. Arrives in the Umeda hub |
| **Hankyu Limited Express** | Kyoto-Kawaramachi → Osaka-Umeda | 45 min | ¥410 | About every 10 min. The station is a 10-minute walk across the river from Gion |
| Shinkansen | Kyoto Station → Shin-Osaka | 15 min | ¥1,450 | Then 10–15 min more by subway to Namba |

One IC card (ICOCA or Suica) works on JR, Hankyu and the Osaka Metro. Our [Kyoto transport guide](/blog/plan/transport-kyoto) covers where to get one.

<TipBox type="warning" title="TWO TRAIN TRAPS">
<strong>The JR local train.</strong> It costs the same ¥580 as the Special Rapid and takes about 45 minutes. Let it leave and wait for the next Special Rapid.<br /><br />
<strong>The Shinkansen.</strong> The fare is about 2.5 times higher, and once you add the subway from Shin-Osaka the time saved is about 15 minutes. That holds even with a JR Pass.
</TipBox>

## What to Do

- **Dotonbori:** the canal-side heart of Namba, with neon signs, the Glico running man and street food at every step. Allow 90 minutes to walk both banks and eat. Try takoyaki at Aizuya or Kukuru and okonomiyaki at Mizuno. The moving crab sign of Kani Doraku marks the seafood restaurants.
- **Kuromon Ichiba Market:** a 10-minute walk from Dotonbori. Fresh fish, shellfish and fruit, much of it grilled on the spot. Go between 9am and 1pm, because fish stalls start closing in the afternoon.
- **Shinsekai and Tsutenkaku Tower:** a retro working-class district about 10 minutes away by subway. This is the home of *kushikatsu*, deep-fried skewers. The rule in every shop: never dip twice in the shared sauce. The tower costs ¥1,500 to go up.
- **Osaka Castle:** a rebuilt castle in a large park. The outside is impressive and the inside is a modern museum (¥1,200). Allow 90 minutes if history interests you.

## Half-Day Route

1. **09:30 Kyoto Station.** JR Special Rapid to Osaka Station, then the Midosuji subway line to Namba (about 15 minutes).
2. **10:30 Dotonbori.** 90 minutes. Walk both sides of the canal and start with takoyaki. ¥1,000–2,000 in street food.
3. **12:00 Lunch.** Okonomiyaki, kushikatsu or ramen for ¥1,200–2,000. The famous shops queue from 12:00 to 13:30, so pick your second choice.
4. **13:15 Kuromon Market.** 45 minutes. Grilled scallops, uni, fresh fruit.
5. **14:15 Shinsekai.** 60 minutes. Subway from Nippombashi. Skip it if you would rather have more time on the canal.
6. **16:00 Train back.** You are at Kyoto Station around 16:45.

For a full day, see Osaka Castle in the morning before Dotonbori and stay on for dinner.
