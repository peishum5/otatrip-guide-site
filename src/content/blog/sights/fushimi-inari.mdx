Fushimi Inari is free, open 24 hours and five minutes by train from Kyoto Station. That makes it Kyoto's most visited sight, and also the easiest one to have almost to yourself.

## The Short Version

- **Go before 8am or after 5pm.** From 9am to 4pm the lower gates are shoulder to shoulder.
- **Take the JR Nara Line to Inari.** 5 minutes, ¥150, and the shrine is across the street.
- **It costs nothing.** There is no entry fee anywhere on the mountain.
- **Turn around at Yotsutsuji**, 30–40 minutes up, unless you want the full 2–3 hour loop.
- **Skip the bus.** It stops far from the shrine and runs less often than the trains.

## Getting There

- **From Kyoto Station:** JR Nara Line to Inari Station. The shrine is directly across the street.
- **From Gion-Shijo:** Keihan Main Line to Fushimi-Inari Station, then a 5-minute walk. The better choice from eastern Kyoto.
- **By taxi:** about ¥1,500–¥2,000 from central Kyoto. With trains this good, you rarely need one.

## How Far to Climb

| Section | Time from the bottom | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Lower gates | 0–20 min | The famous torii tunnels. Every classic photo is taken here |
| Yotsutsuji junction | 30–40 min | The first clearing, with views over southern Kyoto. Most people turn back here |
| Summit loop | 2–3 hours round trip | Quiet forest and dozens of small sub-shrines. The summit (233 m) has little view |

The lower paths are paved. Above Yotsutsuji they turn into rougher forest tracks and the crowds thin out fast.

The shrine dates to 711 and honors Inari, the god of rice and business. Each gate was donated by a person or a company, and the stone foxes are Inari's messengers.

<TipBox type="warning" title="GOLDEN WEEK AND NEW YEAR">
Golden Week (late April to early May) and the New Year holidays are the busiest days of the year. If you visit then, arrive at or before sunrise.
</TipBox>

## Good to Know

- **Shoes:** the summit path is 4 km of uneven stone steps. Wear comfortable walking shoes.
- **Toilets:** at the main shrine building and at Yotsutsuji junction.
- **Tripods:** not allowed in the gate tunnels during busy hours.
- **Food:** the stalls near the entrance open around 9am. Try the inari sushi on your way down.
