teamLab Biovortex Kyoto opened in October 2025, a 7-minute walk south of Kyoto Station. It is teamLab's largest museum in Japan: more than 50 artworks across about 10,000 square meters.

## The Short Version

- **Worth it?** Yes. We went and could have stayed all day. Every room has small hidden tricks, and each work has a clear idea behind it.
- **Plan on 2.5 hours.** There is no time limit once you are in.
- **Book ahead.** Tickets are timed and weekends sell out.
- **Wear sneakers.** You cannot enter the climbing area in heels or sandals.
- **Great with kids.** A whole zone is built for climbing, balancing and sliding.
- **Open until 21:00** (last entry 19:30). It fits after a day of temples, or on a rainy day.

## What It Is Like Inside

The first thing we noticed was the size. You keep turning corners into new rooms, and almost nothing is a screen on a wall. Many works are physical things in the space: a huge sculpture made of bubbles floating in mid-air, spheres of light that change shape, real moss and plants mixed with digital light.

Look closely and stay a while. Most works react to people, and the details change as you move. The museum's theme is how things exist only through their surroundings, like a whirlpool in water. You do not need to know that to enjoy it, but it explains why the works keep shifting.

Some works change with the real seasons, so the flowers you see in November are different from those in spring.

<VideoClip class="max-w-xs mx-auto my-7" src="/videos/blog/teamlab-tea.mp4" poster="/images/blog/inline/teamlab-tea.jpg" alt="Small glasses of drinks glowing in different colours on a dark counter among crystals" caption="Even the drinks glow, each in its own colour." />

A tea house inside the art, Teahouse in a Sea of Light, opened on 20 November 2026. The tea starts to glow as it is made, and it also serves gelato and sweets.

## With Kids: Athletics Forest

This is the part that surprised us most. Athletics Forest is a large, multi-level space you move through with your whole body:

- **Aerial Climbing through a Flock of Colored Birds:** climb through the space among moving birds of light.
- **Balance Stepping Stones in the Invisible World:** keep your balance stone to stone.
- **Sliding through the Fruit Field:** a slide where fruit grows as you go down.
- **Graffiti Nature:** children draw a creature on paper and it comes to life in the room.

In **Future Park**, the creature your child draws in Sketch Ocean swims on the walls with everyone else's. At the shop, that drawing can be printed onto a badge, T-shirt or tote bag.

<TipBox type="local" title="FAMILY NOTES">
<strong>No strollers inside.</strong> They go in the luggage room at the entrance.<br /><br />
<strong>Under-12s need an adult</strong> (18 or older), with one adult for every three children.<br /><br />
There is a nursing and diaper-changing room in Athletics Forest. Some works have height or age limits.

## Tickets

| Ticket | Price |
|---|---|
| Adults, 18+ | ¥3,800 in Nov 2026 (varies by date and time) |
| Ages 13–17 | ¥2,800 |
| Ages 4–12 | ¥1,800 |
| Age 3 and under | Free |
| Visitors with disabilities (+1 companion) | ¥1,900 (varies by date) |
| Flexible Pass (any time that day) | ¥12,000 |

- **Buy on the [official ticket site](https://kyoto.tickets.teamlab.art/#/?lang=en).** Tickets at the door cost ¥200 more and may be sold out.
- **Tickets go on sale around the 1st of the month, two months ahead.** Book as soon as your date is set, especially for a weekend.
- **Your ticket has an entry time, not an exit time.** Stay as long as you like.
- **No refunds,** but tickets from the official site can change date up to three times, until 2 hours before your entry time.

## Before You Go

- **Shoes:** wear sneakers. Heels, sandals and clogs are not allowed in Athletics Forest. A few rental sneakers exist, but sizes are limited.
- **Clothes:** some floors are mirrors, so skirts are not ideal. White or light solid colors look best in photos, because the art reflects on you.
- **Bags:** nothing over 50 cm goes inside. Leave suitcases at [Kyoto Station](/blog/plan/kyoto-luggage-storage) before you walk over.
- **Photos:** allowed, and you can post them. No flash, no tripods or selfie sticks of 30 cm or more.
- **Food:** no food or gum inside. Drinks are fine only with a cap. There is no restaurant, only vending machines and the tea house (10:30–20:45).
- **Light and sound:** some works flash and are loud. People sensitive to light should know this before booking.

## Getting There

- **On foot:** 7 minutes from the **Hachijo East Exit** of JR Kyoto Station (the south, Shinkansen side). See the [Kyoto Station guide](/blog/plan/kyoto-station-guide) for finding the exit.
- **From Gion:** Keihan Line from Gion-Shijo to Shichijo (3 minutes), then 11 minutes on foot. A taxi is about 15 minutes.
- **Address:** 21-5 Higashikujo Higashi-Iwamotocho, Minami-ku, Kyoto. There is no parking.
