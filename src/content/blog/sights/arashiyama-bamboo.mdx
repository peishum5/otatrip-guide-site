The bamboo grove is a 500-metre path that takes ten minutes to walk, and by 9am it is too crowded to photograph. Visiting it well is all about timing.

## The Short Version

- **Arrive before 8am.** The path never closes and is nearly empty at that hour, even on weekends.
- **It is free.** No gate, no ticket.
- **Avoid 10am to 3pm.** If you cannot come early, come after 4pm.
- **Take the JR Sagano Line.** 17 minutes from Kyoto Station, then a 10-minute walk.
- **Plan more than the bamboo.** The grove itself takes 15 minutes, so add Tenryu-ji or Jojakko-ji.

## Getting There

- **JR Sagano Line (San'in Line)** to Saga-Arashiyama Station: 17 minutes from Kyoto Station and the fastest way. The grove entrance is a 10-minute walk from the station.
- **Keifuku Arashiyama Line** to Arashiyama Station: slower but scenic. It leaves from Shijo-Omiya in central Kyoto.
- **Hankyu** to Katsura, then change to the Arashiyama Line.
- **City Bus 28** from Kyoto Station: about 50 minutes, slower and less reliable than the train.

IC cards (Suica, ICOCA) work on all of these.

## Beating the Crowds

  <CrowdMeter label="After 4pm" level={3} note="Quieter than midday, pleasant afternoon light" />

- **Pick a weekday.** Weekends from 9am to 4pm are slow-moving queues.
- **Walk the whole path.** Most people stop halfway for photos. The far end, toward Okochi Sanso villa, is noticeably quieter. Allow 45 minutes there and back.
- **Hope for cloud.** Even light makes the bamboo glow green. Midday sun throws harsh shadows.

The stalks reach 10–15 metres, and the sound of wind moving through them is on the official list of 100 Soundscapes of Japan. There are no facilities on the path.

<TipBox type="warning" title="CHERRY BLOSSOM AND AUTUMN WEEKENDS">
In sakura season (late March to early April) and autumn foliage season (mid-November), 8am may already be too late on a weekend. Aim for 6:30–7am, or come on a weekday.
</TipBox>

## What to See Nearby

| Sight | Cost | Why go |
|---|---|---|
| Tenryu-ji Temple | ¥500 garden, ¥800 with the buildings | A UNESCO World Heritage Zen garden, with a pond set against the Arashiyama hills |
| Nonomiya Shrine | Free | A small shrine in the bamboo just before the grove entrance, linked to *The Tale of Genji* |
| Jojakko-ji Temple | ¥600 | A mossy hillside temple that tour groups skip. Ten minutes up to the pagoda for views over Arashiyama |
| Togetsukyo Bridge | Free | Arashiyama's signature bridge, a 10-minute walk south |

Go to Jojakko-ji straight after the bamboo, while the area is still quiet.
