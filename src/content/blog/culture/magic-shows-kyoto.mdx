Kyoto is not a big-theater magic city. What it does well is small and close: counter magic in Gion, one immersive show, and one high-end dinner with magic.

## The Short Version

- **Three venues are worth knowing**, all in or near Gion and Kawaramachi.
- **Svengali** for a full show night: ¥10,000–¥15,000, ages 16 and up.
- **Magic Bar i&i** for classic close-up magic over drinks in Gion: ¥7,000.
- **Benizakuro** for a celebration: kaiseki dinner with magic, ¥50,000 plus drinks.
- **Reserve the show first, then plan dinner around it.** Every room is small.

## Svengali: The Full Show

[Official site](https://www.svengali.jp/en/) | [Google Maps](https://maps.google.com/?q=PLEXUS+7th+Floor+Bizenjimacho+Nakagyo+Kyoto)

- **What it is:** an immersive masked cabaret that blends magic, circus, visual theater, music and storytelling. A real production, not bar entertainment.
- **Show time:** 8:30 pm, 90 minutes.
- **Tickets:** ¥10,000 standard, ¥15,000 premium. Cocktails are included.
- **Age rule:** 16+ only.
- **Where:** central Kyoto, near Kawaramachi and easy to reach from Gion.
- **Best for:** couples and anyone who wants a proper evening event. The show is built to work for international visitors.

## Magic Bar i&i: Close-Up Magic in Gion

[Official site](https://magicbar-i-and-i.jp/) | [Google Maps](https://maps.google.com/?q=347-155+Kitagawa+Higashiyama-ku+Kyoto)

- **What it is:** close-up counter magic with a drink in your hand. It has been in Gion for about 30 years.
- **Format:** 90 minutes of all-you-can-drink plus magic for ¥7,000. The cheapest of the three, and your drinks are covered.
- **Hours:** 8:00 pm – 2:00 am.
- **Where:** a 7-minute walk from Gion-Shijo Station.
- **Seat:** ask for the counter, not a table. The magic happens at the bar, within arm's reach.
- **Best for:** an easy stop after dinner in Gion. It is the most flexible of the three for families.

## Benizakuro: Dinner and Magic

[Official site](https://www.benizakuro.com/en) | [Google Maps](https://maps.google.com/?q=347-114+Gionmachi+Kitagawa+Higashiyama-ku+Kyoto)

- **What it is:** a 7-dish kaiseki dinner with a 30-minute magic show, in a small private-feeling room in Gion.
- **Price:** ¥50,000 per person, plus drinks.
- **Where:** a 5-minute walk from Gion-Shijo Station, 3 minutes from Yasaka Shrine.
- **Best for:** an anniversary, proposal or birthday, when the food and the setting matter as much as the magic.

<TipBox type="warning" title="BENIZAKURO IS NOT A DROP-IN VENUE">
A reservation is required, so do not arrive without one. The kaiseki menu is set, so confirm dietary requirements when you book.
</TipBox>

## A Good Evening Plan

1. Early evening walk through [Gion](/blog/neighborhoods/gion).
2. Dinner nearby.
3. The show or the magic bar.
4. A short walk afterward around Yasaka Shrine, the Shirakawa canal or the Kamo River. The canal is almost empty after 10pm, so leave 20 minutes for it.
