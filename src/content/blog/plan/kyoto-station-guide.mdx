Kyoto Station serves five rail operators and hides its bus terminal behind a fifteen-storey atrium. Know which side you need and which stand your bus leaves from, and you stop losing twenty minutes every time you pass through.

## The Short Version

- **Use the Karasuma (north) exit** for buses, the subway, taxis and downtown. It is the right exit about 90% of the time.
- **Use the Hachijo (south) exit** only for the Kintetsu line to Nara, the airport limousine bus and hotels on the south side.
- **Bus 206 from stand D2** goes to Kiyomizudera and Gion. **Bus 205 from stand B3** goes to Kinkakuji.
- **Allow 15 minutes** to change between regular JR lines and the Shinkansen.
- **Coin lockers fill by 10am in peak season.** Crosta Kyoto on the B1 floor is the staffed backup.

<TipBox type="warning" title="THE WRONG-SIDE TRAP">
Crossing between the two exits takes 10–15 minutes with luggage, and picking the wrong side is the most common mistake here. The airport limousine bus drops you at Hachijo, the wrong side for the subway, city buses and downtown. Take a taxi from the Hachijo rank instead of dragging suitcases through the concourse.
</TipBox>

## The Bus Terminal

- **Where:** directly outside the Karasuma exit. Each stand has a letter and a number, and a digital board with the next departures.
- **Do not board a "Rapid" 205** for Kinkakuji. It skips the stop.
- **Board here and you get a seat.** In peak season, people joining further along the route usually stand.

## Trains: Who Runs What

- **JR local lines:** main ground-floor gates.
- **Tokaido Shinkansen:** its own gates on the south side, with a separate ticket.
- **Haruka airport express:** platform 30, at the far west end. A long walk from the central concourse.
- **Kintetsu line (for Nara):** a separate station on the Hachijo side.
- **Karasuma subway line:** underground, entered from the Karasuma side.

**Walking time for transfers:** 15 minutes between regular JR and the Shinkansen, and 10 minutes to the Haruka platform, the Kintetsu or the subway.

## Luggage, Food and a Free View

- **Coin lockers:** about ¥400 small, ¥500 medium and ¥700 large. Most take IC cards. Over 1,500 are on the Hachijo side, with more near the Karasuma exit and in the Porta underground mall.
- **Crosta Kyoto (B1 floor):** staffed storage for bags too big for a locker, with same-day delivery to Kyoto hotels. More in [Luggage in Kyoto](/blog/plan/kyoto-luggage-storage).
- **Food:** Kyoto Ramen Koji on the 10th floor, the Isetan food hall in the basement and the Porta mall.
- **Free view:** the Skyway walkway and rooftop terrace on the upper floors look north over the city. Cheaper than Kyoto Tower across the road.
