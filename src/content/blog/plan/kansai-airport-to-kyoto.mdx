Kansai Airport (KIX) sits on an artificial island about 100 km from Kyoto. For almost everyone the answer is one direct train, and the only real trap is landing late.

## The Short Version

- **Take the JR Haruka.** It runs direct to Kyoto Station in 75–80 minutes for ¥3,060, with luggage racks and no traffic.
- **Take the limousine bus only if it stops at your hotel.** It saves a few hundred yen, but traffic can add 45 minutes.
- **The last Haruka leaves at about 22:15.** If you land after 21:30, plan the connection before you fly.
- **Have a Japan Rail Pass?** The Haruka is fully covered.
- **A taxi costs ¥20,000 or more.** Keep it for emergencies.

## Every Route Compared

| Route | Time | Fare | Arrives at | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **JR Haruka limited express** | 75–80 min | ¥3,060 non-reserved / ~¥3,590 reserved | Kyoto Station | Almost everyone |
| Limousine bus | ~100 min (depends on traffic) | ~¥2,600–2,800 | Kyoto Station Hachijo exit | Hotels on the bus route |
| Shared shuttle van (book ahead) | 100–150 min | ~¥4,500–5,000 per person | Your hotel door | Children, elderly parents, three big suitcases |
| Taxi / private transfer | ~90 min | ¥20,000+ | Your hotel door | A family of four landing after the last train |
| Nankai + transfer | 100–120 min | ~¥1,700 | Kyoto Station | Tight budgets only |

## JR Haruka

- **Finding it:** follow the "JR" signs from arrivals. A short covered walkway links Terminal 1 to the station. Trains leave roughly every 30 minutes.
- **Seats:** non-reserved is usually fine. Reserve for a group of three or more, or in Golden Week and at New Year.
- **JR Pass:** exchange or activate it at the JR ticket office in the airport station first. Queues can run 20–30 minutes.
- **Discount:** JR West sells a cheaper Haruka ticket to foreign passport holders. Ask for the current price at the same office.

## Limousine Bus

- **Route:** from outside the arrivals hall to Kyoto Station's Hachijo (south) exit. Some buses continue to a few major hotels. Buy tickets at the machine by the stop.
- **The catch:** Hachijo is the opposite side of the station from the subway and most bus stops. Crossing with suitcases takes 10–15 minutes.

## If You Land Late

After the last Haruka at about 22:15:

1. **A late limousine bus.** Times vary by season, so check this first.
2. **Nankai line to Namba and a night in Osaka.** Often the cheapest sane choice. Kyoto is 45 minutes away the next morning.
3. **A taxi.**

<TipBox type="warning" title="CHECK YOUR LANDING TIME BEFORE YOU BOOK KYOTO">
Immigration and baggage reclaim at KIX regularly take 60–90 minutes after a long-haul flight. If you are scheduled to land after about 21:30, book your first night in Osaka instead of Kyoto. It is cheaper and far less stressful than finding this out at 22:40.
</TipBox>

## Arriving at Kyoto Station

- **Platform:** the Haruka arrives at platform 30, at the far west end. Allow 10 minutes to reach the main concourse.
- **Onward:** the subway, buses and taxis are on the Karasuma (north) side. With luggage, take a taxi to Gion or Higashiyama. The subway reaches downtown Shijo in five minutes.
- **IC card:** buy an ICOCA at a green JR machine before you leave the station. See [How to Get Around Kyoto](/blog/plan/transport-kyoto).
